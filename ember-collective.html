Ember Collective

Web Design Los Angeles, CA 2023
Scroll

Overview

Ember Collective is an independent group of artists whose old site — a stock portfolio template — did the opposite of what their physical work did: it made everything feel smaller, tighter, generic.

I designed an editorial-style site built around a print-inspired grid: oversized type, deliberate whitespace, and feature spreads that let each artist's work breathe at the scale it deserves. The CMS templates mean the collective can publish new exhibitions themselves without touching a line of code.
