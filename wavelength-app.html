Wavelength App

UI/UX Design Remote Team 2023
Scroll

Overview

Wavelength set out to help independent musicians get discovered without competing against algorithmic playlists built for major labels. The early prototype worked, but it looked and felt like every other cover-art grid on the market.

I took the product from wireframes through a full component-based design system, replacing generic thumbnails with waveform-driven browsing — letting the shape of a track do the work a cover image usually does. Every screen, state, and interaction pattern was documented so engineering could ship it without guessing.
