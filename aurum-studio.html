Aurum Studio

Brand Identity Milan, Italy 2024
Scroll

Overview

Aurum Studio needed a visual language that could sit at the table with the luxury fashion and design houses they wanted as clients — but their existing mark was an afterthought, borrowed from a template and never revisited.

I built the identity around a single restrained gesture: a monogram that reads as a mark at a glance and a wordmark at full size, carried across a warm black-and-gold system that felt considered rather than loud. The system now spans everything from business cards to the studio's own site.
