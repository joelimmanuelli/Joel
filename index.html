ABOUT

Physical products defined by functional restraint and mechanical clarity.

Based in Singapore, I am an industrial designer dedicated to the development of physical products defined by functional restraint, mechanical clarity, and thoughtful detailing. Currently studying Industrial Design at the National University of Singapore (NUS), my work focuses on translating functional necessities into intuitive, tangible objects that hold distinct material and ergonomic value.

My practice is anchored in a strong background in mechanical engineering, which bridges technical discipline and creative problem-solving. This technical grounding informs how I approach design from the inside out, evaluating internal mechanisms, material properties, tolerance considerations, and manufacturing processes alongside form and user experience. Rather than treating aesthetics as surface treatment, I design objects where structure, mechanism, and external geometry work in direct alignment.

Hands-on iteration and physical prototyping sit at the core of my workflow, progressing from early mechanism mockups to high-fidelity fabrication and refined CAD modeling. In collaborative environments, I leverage clear communication and cross-disciplinary dialogue to navigate complex production constraints, delivering robust, carefully crafted products that balance engineering precision with quiet, lasting utility.
