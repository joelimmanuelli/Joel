Verdant Health

Product Design San Francisco, CA 2024
Scroll

Overview

Verdant set out to turn fragmented health data — sleep, activity, symptoms, appointments — into something people would actually open every day, not just after a doctor's visit.

I led the product from early research through a 0-to-1 design process, landing on a calm, legible dashboard that surfaces trends over raw numbers and never asks for more attention than the moment deserves. The system shipped across web and mobile from a single component library.
